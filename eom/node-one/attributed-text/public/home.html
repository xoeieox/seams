Names here are claims. Keys here are proof.

You can claim any name - but you can't forge a signature.

What this is

  • One shared page of text, open to anyone to change.
  • Every word keeps whoever wrote it, in a public, exportable log.
  • Entry is free and instant: apart from a name you choose, it costs less than email.
  • No account, no sign-in: a name you type is a claim; a key you hold is proof.

What this is not

  • Not verified: the only check is this gateway's signature arithmetic at ingest. No third party has verified anything. A checked signature proves control of a key at deposit time - nothing else.
  • No payments. No accounts, no email capture, no KYC, no pricing.
  • No vouches. Nobody attests who is behind a key. The node never blocks a claimed name.
  • Unsigned entries are labeled claims, forever.

What it's meant to be

  • Attribution you can check yourself: the log is a log, not an arbiter.
  • Two people may claim the same name and both be visible, honestly - the log doesn't decide who is real.
  • Proof of authorship, never identity.

How we get there

  • The launcher: keys are put together on your machine, never by this site. The site never holds or intermediates identity.
  • Next: a packaged launcher download, then the consent gate this demo does not exercise yet.
  • Pull the repo, point your agent at AGENTS.md (or use the launcher, or do it by hand), and grow your own node. Then send signed, attributed messages here.

Get proof - the launcher

Keys come from the launcher: take a real Ed25519 keypair on your machine generated by the launcher, the private half never leaves it, and signed deposits check creator_verified true on this site.
